Every video you have ever watched online passed through its hands. **FFmpeg** decodes, encodes, filters, and streams essentially every audio and video format in existence — a quarter-century of C, maintained like couture.

The command line is terse to the point of poetry:

```bash
ffmpeg -i input.mov -c:v libx264 -crf 18 output.mp4
```

- Codecs, containers, and filters beyond counting
- Powers VLC, Chrome, OBS, and half the internet
- One binary, everywhere

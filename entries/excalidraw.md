Precision is not always what an idea needs. **Excalidraw** draws like a pencil — wobbly lines, imperfect circles — and in doing so made diagramming feel like thinking out loud rather than drafting specifications.

Real-time collaboration, an infinite canvas, and an end-to-end encrypted scene format. The tool whiteboards were always pretending to be.

- Hand-drawn aesthetic that lowers the stakes
- Live multiplayer sessions
- Excalidraw+ for teams, core forever open source

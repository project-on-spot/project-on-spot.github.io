One include, and the flat document gains a third dimension. **Three.js** wraps WebGL in vocabulary a designer can think in — scenes, meshes, materials, light — and has quietly dressed the web's most ambitious experiences for over a decade.

It is less a library than a lighting department: shadows fall where physics says they should, and the frame budget holds.

- Scene graph, materials, shaders, and post-processing
- The default instrument of creative coding on the web
- Examples directory that functions as a masterclass

Before dashboards were a product category, they were a craft, and **D3** was the loom. Scales, axes, selections, transitions — a complete grammar for binding data to the DOM, with nothing hidden.

It does not hand you charts. It hands you the means to think in them, which is why every visualization library of consequence since has borrowed its vocabulary.

- Data joins between data and document
- Scales, shapes, zoom, and force layouts as composable modules
- The foundation beneath a decade of data journalism

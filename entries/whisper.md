**Whisper** listens without prejudice: ninety-nine languages, accents, crosstalk, and kitchen noise. Trained on weakly labeled audio at scale, its approach was so simple it reads like a manifesto — stop curating labels, curate the corpus.

The weights and the training code are open, and transcription has never been the same.

- Near-human accuracy across 99 languages
- Robust to accents, background noise, and technical language
- Models from tiny to large, all downloadable

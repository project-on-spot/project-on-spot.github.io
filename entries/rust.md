The borrow checker is the strictest editor in the industry, and the better for it. **Rust** proves performance and safety are not a trade — its compiler refuses the memory errors other languages merely apologize for.

From operating systems to browsers to the toolchain of the web itself, it is the material the next decade is being cut from.

- Ownership and lifetimes as compile-time guarantees
- Zero-cost abstractions, C-class speed
- Cargo: dependency management as a first-class act
